Top Arena

Physics-based ball battle videos, published automatically as YouTube Shorts.

What it is

Top Arena is a private tool that simulates deterministic 2D "ball battle" matches, renders them as short vertical videos and publishes them as YouTube Shorts on its owner's own YouTube channel. Every match is decided by physics: no script and no rigging.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the channel. Top Arena is not a public service, it has no user accounts and nobody else signs in to it. It runs on the owner's private server.

How it uses YouTube

  • Uploads the rendered videos to the owner's channel and schedules their publishing time.
  • Reads the view, like and comment counts of the videos it uploaded, to send the owner a short status report.

Details are in the privacy policy.
